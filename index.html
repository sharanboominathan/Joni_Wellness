The psychology of feeling beautiful

Why a visit to JONI feels so good.

Looking good is more than looks. Here is what research on attraction and confidence suggests, and how we use it for you.

1Halo effect

One polished detail lifts everything

People who look well-groomed are often judged as more confident and capable. A fresh cut or neat nails changes how others see you, and how you walk into the room.

2Social proof

Trusted by hundreds

We trust what many others have already chosen. 5.0 on Google from 371 reviews and 11.5K+ Instagram followers show you are in good company.

3Enclothed cognition

Look the part, feel the part

How we dress and style ourselves shapes how we think and act. A new look lifts your mood and quietly changes how you carry yourself.

4Reciprocity

Listened to before anything else

When someone gives us real attention, we relax and trust them. We start every visit with a conversation, so you get a look that is yours.

5Scarcity

Good chairs fill up

Time slots are limited, and evenings and weekends tend to go first. Message early to get the time that suits you.

6Mere exposure

Familiar faces, better results

We like what we know. Returning to the same stylists means they learn your hair, your skin and your taste, and each visit gets better.
